How to Get Clients.

Beginner Freelancer Guide

The hardest part of freelancing isn't doing the work; it's finding the clients. When you are a beginner, you have no reviews, no portfolio, and no network. Here is the exact strategy to land your first client.

1. Build a Proof of Work (Portfolio)

Clients don't buy promises; they buy results. If you don't have real clients yet, create 3 "fake" projects. Design a mock website, write a sample article, or code a small app. Put them on a clean, fast-loading website.

Need a portfolio? Ocide Coders can build one for you for free.

2. Cold Outreach (The Secret Weapon)

Don't wait for clients to find you. Find businesses on Google Maps or Instagram that have bad websites or poor content. Send them a polite email:

  • Compliment their business.
  • Point out one specific thing you can improve.
  • Offer to do it for free or at a huge discount for the first 3 clients in exchange for a testimonial.
