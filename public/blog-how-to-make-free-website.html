How to Make a Free Website.

A Guide by Ocide Coders

Having a personal website is no longer a luxury; it's a necessity. Whether you are a developer, designer, or writer, your website is your digital handshake. But how do you make one without spending money? Here are the best ways.

1. Use Free Hosting Platforms

You can host static websites for free using services like GitHub Pages, Vercel, or Netlify. You just need to write your HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, push it to a repository, and link it to these services. They will give you a free subdomain (e.g., yourname.github.io).

2. The Problem with Website Builders

While platforms like Wix or WordPress seem free at first, they often lock premium features behind a paywall, show their own ads on your site, or require you to pay for a custom domain. Moreover, their templates can be slow and bloated.

3. The Custom Code Advantage

The best way to get a fast, professional website is to use custom code (HTML/CSS). It loads in milliseconds, is 100% responsive, and has no bloat. If you don't know how to code, you can still get one for free.
