What is Eliget? The Anti-Addiction Social Media

Published by Ocide Coders • October 20, 2026 • 3 min read

In an era where social media platforms are designed to keep you scrolling infinitely, mental fatigue and digital addiction have become a massive global issue. Enter Eliget — a platform built on the exact opposite philosophy.

Eliget is not just another social network. It is an anti-addiction, no-algorithm, text-focused community designed for creators, freelancers, and anyone seeking a distraction-free environment.

Why We Built Eliget

Traditional social media companies make money by keeping your eyes on the screen for as long as possible. They use complex algorithms to show you short videos and controversial posts to trigger dopamine hits. We decided to stop that.

  • No Infinite Short Videos: We don't have a TikTok or Reels feed. Video links are allowed, but they don't autoplay.
  • No Algorithmic Feed: You see posts in chronological order from people you actually follow. You control what you see.
  • Text-First Approach: Thoughtful posts and discussions take priority over clickbait thumbnails.

Features That Respect Your Time

We also built a dedicated Job Board to help freelancers find genuine clients without sifting through spam. Additionally, our platform includes a built-in screen-time warning to remind you to take a break after 20 minutes of continuous use.

Eliget is a product of Ocide Coders, built with the vision of making the internet a more human and less addictive place.
